    06 / QUICK ANSWERS

    Frequently asked questions.

    Does “Recent” mean the sensor is connected right now?

    No. It describes the timestamp of the last saved reading. Use the Monitor sync controls to check the device.

    Are heat-index levels a medical diagnosis or school-closure decision?

    No. They are screening information. Follow school procedures, official guidance, and emergency services when needed.

    Where are my readings saved?

    Readings are stored in this browser. They are not automatically shared with other devices; cloud sync depends on the app’s configuration and internet access.

    Are forecasts measured readings or guarantees?

    No. The published 60-minute forecast is a Python model estimate from the CSV dataset, not a measured future temperature. Actual conditions may differ.

    How can I add a reading?

    Open Sensor Monitor to enter temperature and humidity manually, sync over Wi-Fi using the laptop bridge, or sync directly with a supported ESP32 over Bluetooth in a compatible desktop browser.

    Why am I seeing demo readings?

    SchoolHeat can create clearly marked sample readings to help you preview the interface. Demo readings are excluded from forecast training and validation. Use your own trusted sensor readings when reviewing real conditions.

    How much history does forecasting need?

    It uses the separate six-column CSV dataset in the scheduled Python job. If forecast.json is still pending, the app says the forecast has not been generated. Forecasts should be treated as estimates, not guarantees.

    Do SchoolHeat alerts contact 911 or local responders?

    No. Browser, phone push, and SMS alerts notify configured users only; they do not contact emergency responders. For an emergency, call 911 and follow school procedures.

    Do SMS and phone alerts work offline?

    No. Delivery requires internet access. SMS may remain queued in this browser while offline and can retry after connectivity returns; it is not delivered until the app is online.
